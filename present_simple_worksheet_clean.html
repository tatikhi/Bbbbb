Task 1

1. Fill in the correct form of the verb:

Every morning, Alex ________ (wake) up at 7:00 AM.
Rule: For he / she / it in Present Simple, add -s / -es to the verb.
Answer: wakes (or wakes up)

Task 2

2. Make the sentence negative:

They like playing video games on weekdays.
Rule: Use don't (do not) for I / you / we / they in negative sentences.
Answer: They don't like playing video games on weekdays.

Task 3

3. Form a question for this answer:

"Does your sister live in London?" -> "Yes, she ________."
Rule: Short answer for "Does she...?" is "Yes, she does." / "No, she doesn't."
Answer: does

Task 4

4. Spelling practice (Third person -s/-es/-ies):

Write the 3rd person singular (he/she/it) form of the verb "STUDY":
Rule: Consonant + y changes to -ies (study -> studies).
Answer: studies

Task 5

5. Choose and complete with the adverb of frequency:

My brother __________ (always / to be) late for school!
Rule: Adverbs of frequency come AFTER the verb to be (am / is / are).
Answer: is always
